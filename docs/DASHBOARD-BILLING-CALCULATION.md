# Dashboard billing calculation

The dashboard uses three independent dates:

- `cycle_start`: assigns transactions to an expense month.
- `statement_date`: records when the statement was generated/delivered.
- `due_date`: assigns the bill amount to its original due month.

For selected month M and previous month P:

- Previous month billed (X): sum `bill_amount_minor` for statements whose `cycle_start` is in P and `due_date` is in M. This amount remains fixed in its original due-month overview and is not reduced by payments.
- Unpaid Bill (Y): sum outstanding balances of all statements already generated by today. Outstanding balances include overdue bills and carry forward until payment allocations clear them. A payment reduces only the statement(s) to which it is allocated.
- Current Expenses (Z): sum transactions dated on or before today whose card billing cycle starts in M. Payments never reduce this amount.

Example: HDFC cycle 1 Oct–31 Oct, statement delivered 1 Nov, due 20 Nov. Its billed amount belongs to November's Previous Month Billed (X). Any unpaid balance remains in Unpaid Bill in later months until fully allocated payments clear it; it is not counted again in later months' X.

No data migration is required. Existing statement dates, due dates, and payment allocations are read as stored.
